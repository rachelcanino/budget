How this app works

The app follows your checking account one day at a time, from today through your last listed paycheck. Each paycheck covers the bills and two weeks of spending until the next one lands.

Every time you open it
  1. Log anything you've bought since last time (Spend tab).
  2. Enter what's in your account now (Paycheck tab), at least on paydays.

Between balance updates, the app estimates your balance: it subtracts purchases you log and bills that come due, and adds paychecks that land. Entering your real balance resets the estimate.

What the numbers mean

  • Ends with: what you'll have when the next paycheck lands.
  • Keep (don't spend): money already needed for something coming up, like the next rent check, school fees, or summer.
  • Spare: money nothing upcoming needs, so it's safe to save or use. It stays in your account until you use it, so the same spare shows on later paychecks too. Keep + Spare = Ends with.
  • On its own: one paycheck minus its own bills and spending. Rent checks are usually red here, and the paycheck before them covers the difference.
  • Lowest point: the least you'll have on any day in that period.

Colors

  • Green: you're fine.
  • Amber: you dip below $0 for one day, and the next paycheck fixes it within your bank's grace window, so there's no fee.
  • Red: a real shortfall. Something needs to change.

The label at the top right sums up the whole plan, summer included.

Weekly spending

Each week has a budget for groceries, household, and treats from your weekly spending list. Weeks start Thursday, and each week counts toward the paycheck it starts in, so every paycheck covers exactly two weeks.

  • Spend less: the leftover moves to Spare when the week ends.
  • Finished early: tap "I'm done shopping this week" and the leftover moves to Spare right away. You can also mark a future week done ahead of time, and undo either one.
  • Someone else covered it: log $0 for that category, and its budget for the week moves to Spare.
  • Spend more: the extra comes out of Spare.

Log every purchase, using "Other" for things that aren't on your spending list. Anything you don't log only shows up once you enter your real balance.

On payday

  1. After the paycheck lands, enter your real balance in "In your account now."
  2. Check the card: pay the bills it lists, and note Keep and Spare.
  3. Progress → Save today's check-in. It only needs today's balance entered; it doesn't need any purchases.

Common changes (Edit tab)

  • New monthly bill: Monthly bills → Add bill.
  • Stopping a bill: set its Ends date.
  • Money coming in (an advance or a gift): One-time items, with a negative amount.
  • Loan paid off early: set its Last date to the last payment you made.
  • Paycheck different than expected: Paydays → change the amount.

Good to know

  • Your numbers live only on this phone. Save a backup to iCloud Drive every couple of weeks. Deleting the home screen icon deletes the app's data.
  • Some amounts are estimates (the bonus, summer checks, health insurance). Update them when you see the real numbers.
  • Bills due today count as not yet paid, so entering your balance in the morning is most accurate.
  • The last paycheck in the list shows extra Spare because the plan can't see past it. Ignore that row's Spare.
